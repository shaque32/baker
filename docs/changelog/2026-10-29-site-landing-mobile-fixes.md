## 2026-10-29: Marketing site landing: phone and tablet fixes from a rendered critique

- Changed: On `site/index.html`, fixed what a headless-Chrome pass at 375, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px (light and dark) showed. The "Assumptions" stepper label overflowed into "Checks" at phone width; it now breaks with a soft hyphen ("Assump-tions"). Phones now keep the evidence citations under the ledger rows (`.rows small`), which were hidden below 621px. The pause button keeps its 26px look but has a 44px tap area. The support address is a `mailto:` link in the call to action and footer instead of copy-only text. The stepper panel is a real tabpanel: `display: grid` instead of `display: contents` (which cannot take focus and drops the role in Safari), each tab has `aria-controls`, and `aria-labelledby` follows the active tab in `paint()` in `site/main.js`. "Click a step" became "Choose a step" so it reads right on touch. The `?v=` hashes on every page were updated for the new `styles.css` and `main.js`.
