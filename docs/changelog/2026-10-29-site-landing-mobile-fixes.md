## 2026-10-29: Marketing site landing: phone and tablet fixes from a rendered critique

- Changed: On `site/index.html`, fixed what a headless-Chrome pass at 375, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px (light and dark) showed. The "Assumptions" stepper label overflowed into "Checks" at phone width; it now breaks with a soft hyphen ("Assump-tions"). Phones now keep the evidence citations under the ledger rows (`.rows small`), which were hidden below 621px. The pause button keeps its 26px look but has a 44px tap area. The support address is a `mailto:` link in the call to action and footer instead of copy-only text. The stepper panel is a real tabpanel: `display: grid` instead of `display: contents` (which cannot take focus and drops the role in Safari), each tab has `aria-controls`, and `aria-labelledby` follows the active tab in `paint()` in `site/main.js`. "Click a step" became "Choose a step" so it reads right on touch. Below 761px the affidavit (`.doc`) was hidden, so step 1 said "Mabillon reads the affidavit" with nothing on screen; it now shows in a compact size. The nav, footer, hero and email links keep their look but get an invisible hit area at least 44px tall, and the stepper tabs have a 44px minimum height. The `?v=` hashes on every page were updated for the new `styles.css` and `main.js`.
- Changed (demo page): On `site/demo.html` and `site/demo-thanks.html`, the support address is a `mailto:` link. The role select now starts on a muted "Choose one" and is required, so it no longer silently submits "Forensic examiner"; the Worker already accepts an empty role. Submit reads "Request a demo", the title is "Book a demo · Mabillon" in sentence case, form fields are at least 44px tall and the privacy link under the form has a 44px tap area.
- Changed (demo form errors and sending): The form Worker (`workers/demo-form/src/index.js`) no longer answers problems with its own unstyled page. A missing or invalid field, the rate limit, an unreadable post, a failed send or a missing `NOTIFY_TO` now redirect 303 to `demo.html?error=<code>` (plus `fields=` for invalid fields), and the old "Form not set up yet" wording is gone. A new `site/demo.js` (loaded by `demo.html` and `demo-thanks.html`, allowed by the existing `script-src 'self'`) shows the message at the top of the form in the site's style, marks the bad fields, and refills what the visitor typed from a copy kept in that browser tab only (sessionStorage), deleted on the thanks page or when the tab closes. On submit the button reads "Sending…" and is disabled, so a slow send can't go out twice; it resets when the page is restored with the back button. `site/privacy.html` now says the browser keeps that tab-only copy. `tests/test_site_assets.py` also checks the `?v=` on `demo.js`.
