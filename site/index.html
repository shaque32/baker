One claim, start to finish

Scroll and the screen builds the check piece by piece. Made-up names and data.

  1. 1

    The claim

    Mabillon reads the affidavit and proposes its claims one at a time. You accept, edit or remove each one.

  2. 2

    What it assumes

    Each claim is split into the facts it depends on: who sent it, when, and what it said.

  3. 3

    The checks

    Code checks the sender, the time and the time zone. Any quote is matched word for word. A model labels records, never the claim.

  4. 4

    Your call

    Nothing is supported until you accept the evidence. Your decision and reason go into the audit log under your name.

  5. 5

    When a fact fails

    A failed check on a core fact contradicts the claim directly, with the check shown. You can still override it.

Rules decide. The model never does.

Versioned rules set every status, and the version goes into each report. You can override any status.

Supported

Only after you accept the evidence behind it.

Contradicted

When a code check on a core fact fails. A model's “contradicts” counts only after you confirm it.

Unproven

Everything else, with what is still missing.

Built to be re-checked

Every status traces back to the record. Every step is logged, and the whole case can be re-run by the other side's examiner.

Inputs hashed

Every file opens read-only, and its SHA-256 goes into the report.

Tamper-evident log

A hash-chained audit log, checked each time a case opens. The report prints its last hash.

Quotes verified

A quote a model points to is checked against the record. One that does not match is thrown away.

Careful about absence

“Not found” never means “did not happen.” It says what was searched and what the source could show.

Identities stay proposed

A number or handle is linked to a person only when you confirm it.

A bundle to re-check

Where it stands

Stage
Alpha, opening to a small number of pilot examiners. No pilot has started yet.
Testing
Tested on synthetic cases only. No accuracy figure for real cases yet.
Formats
Tested on real-format Cellebrite UFDR exports from public test phones (Reader 7.48). Other Cellebrite versions are not yet tested. The Cellebrite Excel and AXIOM readers have not yet been tested on real exports.
Won't do
Give an opinion about any person, extract phones, or replace Cellebrite, AXIOM or your own review. No speed or accuracy figure until it is measured on real extractions.
Attorneys
Mabillon is built for the forensic expert you retain. To bring it into a case, ask us about a pilot together with your expert.
