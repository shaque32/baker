Why it exists

The affidavit quotes a line. The phone holds the conversation.

Checking a claim by hand means scrolling thousands of records for the few that matter, then reading what came before and after. Mabillon searches for the records behind each claim, puts the candidates in front of you, and shows what the document left out around every quote it can place. It can miss a record, so your own search still counts.

How it works

From the government's document to a status for every claim

  1. 01

    Reads what you have

    The affidavit or report as a PDF, and the extraction reports: Cellebrite Excel, PDF and UFDR, and Magnet AXIOM XML and Excel. Each file opens read-only, and its SHA-256 is recorded.

  2. 02

    Splits it into claims

    A local model proposes claims one at a time. You accept, edit or remove each one and add your own. The claim list is yours.

  3. 03

    Tests each claim

    Code checks sender, account, time and time zone, weekday, counts, and what a source can and cannot show. A model labels individual records, never the claim.

  4. 04

    You decide what counts

    Fixed, versioned rules give each claim a status from your decisions and the checks. Every decision goes into the audit log under your name.

Rules, not the model

The model never decides a verdict

Three statuses, set by rules that carry a version number into every report.

Supportedconfirmed by you

When you accept the evidence behind it, or set the status yourself. A model's label alone never makes a claim supported.

Contradictedby a failed check

When a code check on one of the claim's core facts fails, such as a wrong sender or a time outside the stated window. A model's "contradicts" counts only after you confirm it.

Unprovenuntil shown

Everything else, with the records found and what is still missing. You can override any status.

Every quote checked word for word

model: “bring it tonight” → found in Chats, row 212 · kept
model: “bring it to me tonight” → not in the record · thrown away

A quote a model attributes to a record is verified by code before it is stored. One that does not match is discarded.

What it won't do

Your opinion stays yours

Decide for youNo opinion and no conclusion about any person.
Replace your toolsIt does not extract phones or recover data, and it does not replace Cellebrite, AXIOM or your own review.
Fill gaps with guessesWhen it finds no contact or counts records, it says what it searched and what the source could show. A curated report can never show that something is absent.
Promise numbersNo speed or accuracy figure is published until it is measured on real extractions.

Where it stands
