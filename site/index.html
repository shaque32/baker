Built to be re-checked

Every status traces back to the record. Every step is logged, and the whole case can be re-run by the other side's examiner.

Inputs hashed

Tamper-evident log

Careful about absence

Where it stands

Stage
Alpha, opening to a small number of pilot examiners. No pilot has started yet.
Testing
Tested on synthetic cases only. No accuracy figure for real cases yet.
Formats
Tested on real-format Cellebrite UFDR exports from public test phones (Reader 7.48). Other Cellebrite versions are not yet tested. The Cellebrite Excel and AXIOM readers have not yet been tested on real exports.
Won't do
Give an opinion about any person, extract phones, or replace Cellebrite, AXIOM or your own review. No speed or accuracy figure until it is measured on real extractions.
