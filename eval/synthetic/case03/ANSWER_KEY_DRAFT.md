# case03 answer key: DRAFT for Arsh's line-by-line review

**Not gold.** The case03 builder proposed every verdict below. Arsh decides each one.
Approved lines get copied into `eval/gold/case03/gold.jsonl` by a human, with
`labeled_by` set to the approver. Generated by `eval/synthetic/case03_generate.py` from
`case03_story.py` and `case03_key.py`; edit those, not this file.

case03 measures a challenge step: a search for counter-evidence on claims that look
close to supported. It is not part of `make eval` or any gate.

Draft split: 6 supported, 2 contradicted, 9 unproven; 2 claims need both phones.

Groups:

1. counter-evidence code can find
2. counter-evidence only a word-variant search finds
3. clean, supported, with near-miss decoys
4. defined terms, alias or relative date

Shared assumption: Item 1 is used by ORLOV and Item 2 by DIAZ, so the owner accounts on each phone speak for them unless the case shows otherwise. The key treats this as given, as the affidavit does. It is not proven by the data, and claims C03, C11 and C17 exist to test where it breaks.

Labeling rules:

1. A claim is contradicted only when observed data rules it out: the quoted message exists with another sender, or at another time, and no copy fits the claim (C06, C08).
2. Counter-evidence that makes another account of events more likely, without ruling the claim out, complicates the claim and leaves it unproven (every group 1 and group 2 claim).
3. A meaning claim is supported only when the case itself fixes the meaning; an agent's reading of slang or of a translation is inferred, not observed.
4. Dates and times are judged on the phone's own clock (America/New_York), never on a printed UTC value. Item 1 prints UTC+0; Item 2 prints local time.
5. Defined terms are read as the affidavit defines them: the TARGET PHONE is Item 1, PASHA and SUBJECT-1 are ORLOV, SUBJECT-2 is DIAZ, the SEARCH is the March 14, 2026 search.

**Fixed retrieval** means `core/audit/retrieval.py` (RETRIEVER_VERSION 0.2.0) as the
pipeline runs it, k = 20 per assumption, on the draft assumption sheet. Its reach per
assumption: records inside the window that pass the party, phone and channel filters;
the nearest such record just before and just after the window; quoted text within a day
of it; a quoted message's neighbours in its thread; a record's copy on the other phone.
Contact entries only for an assumption with no window and no channel. The context the
stance labeler reads around a message is not retrieval and is not counted here.

**Directions** are those of `core/hypotheses/explain.py` (VERSION 1.0.0): other_user,
clock, day_boundary, other_app, app_quiet, curated, same_words. Group 1 names the one
that should find each counter-record; the builder did not read or use any challenge
step code.
